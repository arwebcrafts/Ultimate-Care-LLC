Frequently Asked Questions

Common Placement Questions

1. What type of home is Ultimate Home Care LLC?
Ultimate Home Care LLC is an IDD/APDD Waiver Home and Private Pay group home supporting individuals with intellectual and developmental disabilities in a safe, warm, and home-like setting in Anchorage, Alaska.
2. What services are provided for residents?
We provide meal preparation (at least 3 nutritious meals a day with snacks), dressing & personal hygiene, household chores, appointment scheduling, social skill building, assistance with personal care & activities of daily living, medical transportation, daily mind-stimulating activities, and community engagement.
3. Do you provide transportation to medical appointments?
Yes! We provide transportation to medical appointments, dental visits, clinical therapy sessions, and healthcare visits, accompanied by caring staff to ensure safety and punctuality.
4. Are private bedrooms available?
Yes, we offer comfortable, fully furnished private bedrooms with natural window light, storage dressers, and easy access to accessible bathrooms.
5. How do I schedule a visit or inquire about placement?
You can reach us directly by phone at +1 (907) 339-1553 or email smultimate.care6@gmail.com. We are located at 9715 New Haven Loop Anchorage, Alaska 99507.
